# MailCraft Global Email Compliance Architecture

## 1. Regulatory Frameworks

MailCraft incorporates built-in, automated compliance validation to protect guest users and brands from violating global anti-spam and consumer data protection regulations:

1. **CAN-SPAM Act (United States)**: Controlling the Assault of Non-Slick Marketing Act (15 U.S.C. 7701).
2. **GDPR & UK GDPR (European Union / United Kingdom)**: General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679).
3. **DPDP Act 2023 & TRAI Regulations (India)**: Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and Telecom Commercial Communications Customer Preference Regulations (TCCCPR).
4. **CASL (Canada)**: Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (S.C. 2010, c. 23).

---

## 2. CAN-SPAM Compliance Checklist

Every commercial promotional message generated by MailCraft complies with CAN-SPAM requirements:
- **Rule 1: No Deceptive Header Information**: The `From:`, `To:`, and routing information accurately identify the sender. Domain spoofing is prohibited.
- **Rule 2: Accurate Subject Lines**: The subject line must honestly reflect the promotional contents of the email body. Deceptive clickbait (e.g., prefixing promotional emails with `Re:` or `Fwd:` when no prior conversation exists) triggers validation warnings.
- **Rule 3: Clear Identification as an Advertisement**: Promotional emails must be recognizable as advertisements.
- **Rule 4: Valid Physical Postal Address**: The email footer must include a valid physical postal address (street address, P.O. Box, or registered company address) defined in Brand Settings.
- **Rule 5: Conspicuous Opt-Out Mechanism**: The footer must include a clear, accessible unsubscribe link that functions for at least 30 days after dispatch.
- **Rule 6: Prompt Opt-Out Honor**: MailCraft processes unsubscribes **immediately in real time**, well within CAN-SPAM's 10-business-day legal requirement.

---

## 3. GDPR & Data Privacy (EU / UK)

Under GDPR, sending commercial marketing requires a lawful basis—typically **Freely Given, Specific, Informed, and Unambiguous Consent (Article 6(1)(a) & Article 7)**:
- **No Pre-Ticked Checkboxes**: Opt-in consent must be an affirmative action. MailCraft's contact import engine requires the user to explicitly confirm that recipients granted opt-in consent.
- **Proof of Consent**: MailCraft records `consentSource`, `consentDate`, and `ipAddress` when contacts are imported or captured.
- **Right to Withdraw**: Subscribers can withdraw consent at any time via the one-click unsubscribe mechanism.
- **Right to Erasure (Article 17)**: Deleting a contact removes personal identifiers from the audience table. If the contact had unsubscribed, an anonymous cryptographic hash (`emailHash`) is retained in the suppression list to ensure the user cannot be re-imported or emailed by mistake.

## 4. Indian DPDP Act 2023 & TRAI Commercial Communications

For communications targeting individuals in India:
- **DPDP Act 2023 Notice & Consent**: Processing personal data for marketing requires explicit notice specifying the purpose of processing.
- **Consent Manager / Withdrawal**: Recipients have a statutory right to revoke marketing consent. MailCraft suppression records guarantee immediate cessation.
- **TRAI Header / Sender ID Alignment**: Telecommunication and SMS/email marketing regulations require registered sender brand names and commercial communication headers matching business registration.
- **Timing Restrictions**: Commercial marketing should avoid unsocial dispatch windows (TRAI guidelines advise sending between 09:00 and 21:00 recipient local time).

## 5. Automated Pre-Send Compliance Validation Engine

Before any campaign can transition from `draft` to `sending` or `scheduled`, MailCraft executes the automated **Compliance Validator** (`/api/v1/compliance/validate` and `apps/web/lib/compliance-validator.ts`):

| Rule ID | Severity | Validation Check | Action If Failed |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| `RULE_SENDER_IDENTITY` | `critical` | Valid sender name and email configured in Brand Settings. | **Blocks Campaign Send** |
| `RULE_SENDER_OTP_VERIFIED` | `critical` | Sender email identity confirmed via one-time passkey verification. | **Blocks Campaign Send** |
| `RULE_SUBJECT_ACCURACY` | `critical` | Subject line non-empty and devoid of deceptive prefixes (`Re:`, `Fwd:`) without prior thread context. | **Blocks Campaign Send** |
| `RULE_UNSUBSCRIBE_MECHANISM` | `critical` | Template HTML contains verified unsubscribe token tag (`{{unsubscribe_url}}`). | **Blocks Campaign Send** |
| `RULE_PHYSICAL_POSTAL_ADDRESS`| `critical` | Physical postal address present in brand profile or email footer. | **Blocks Campaign Send** |
| `RULE_OPT_IN_CONSENT` | `critical` | Targeted audience confirmed to have explicit opt-in consent status. | **Blocks Campaign Send** |
| `RULE_SUPPRESSION_CHECK` | `high` | Pre-flight audit checking for suppressed, hard-bounced, or complaint emails. | **Auto-Excludes Suppressed Contacts** |
| `RULE_SAFE_REDIRECT_URLS` | `high` | Verifies all template links use secure `http:` or `https:` protocols. | **Flags Insecure Links** |
| `RULE_PROVIDER_HEALTH` | `medium` | Checks that connected sending account is in healthy, non-paused standing. | **Advisory Warning** |
| `RULE_TIMEZONE_TIMING` | `low` | Suggests optimal dispatch times to avoid late-night spam triggers. | **Advisory Recommendation** |

## 6. Audit Trail & Legal Log Retention

MailCraft maintains an immutable audit trail of compliance events:
- `unsubscribe_events`: Records `recipientId`, `campaignId`, `token`, `timestamp`, `ipHash`, and `userAgent`.
- `webhook_event_logs`: Retains raw provider webhook payloads with delivery timestamps.
- `suppressions`: Logs the original source and justification (`source`, `reason`, `auditTrail`) for every suppressed record. Suppressions for spam complaints and hard bounces cannot be purged without administrative justification.
